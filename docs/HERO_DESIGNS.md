# Hero designs

Proposed for your review. Every name, costume and story here is original, and all names are provisional. Designs take the broad feel of a shinobi series (elements, headbands, masks, capes, symbolic elemental auras). **No separate pets, spirit animals, or companions are permitted in hero visuals or gameplay**, and no character, symbol or name from any existing series.

Only Legendaries have more than one look: their four forms are the four evolutions, with the same face and build throughout.

Heroes not yet in the summon pool are marked **not summonable yet**. Their numbers are test values, and adding them changes the odds, so that is your call. See the sheets in `docs/hero_designs/`.

## Legendary

### Solar Beast Sage
Fighter, Solar. A wandering teacher who mastered an ancient sun-beast martial-arts tradition; the beast is represented by symbolic armor and solar energy, never a companion. Warm, loud, and far stronger than he lets on.

- **Base, Wandering Sage:** Plain orange and grey training clothes, rope belt, a modest outline.
- **Evo I, Dawn Disciple:** A layered robe over the gi and a faint warm glow at the hands.
- **Evo II, Beast-Bound Master:** Bronze bracers, clan sash, and a stylized sun-beast emblem on his gauntlet.
- **Evo III, Celestial Guardian:** A radiant armoured mantle and sun disc behind the same weathered face, with a much brighter solar aura around the hero, not a separate creature.

### Thunder Empress
Control, Lightning. A storm-court apprentice who rose to rule the sky over her mountain. Icy, precise, and always the last to raise her voice.

- **Base, Storm Apprentice:** Plain indigo travel wear with a single copper hairpin.
- **Evo I, Court Herald:** A fitted court coat, with small sparks held under tight control.
- **Evo II, Tempest Regent:** Ornate shoulder plates and a clan sigil crackling with charge.
- **Evo III, Thunder Empress:** A tall crowned outline and a mantle of living lightning.

### Stone Titan
Tank, Earth. A quarry labourer who stood his ground in a landslide, and the mountain has stayed with him ever since.

- **Base, Quarry Hand:** A broad labourer in worn leather with a stone maul.
- **Evo I, Wallkeeper:** Fitted slate plates and a calm haze of dust at his feet.
- **Evo II, Ridge Bastion:** Carved clan armour with runes that glow when struck.
- **Evo III, Stone Titan:** Towering living-rock armour around the same weathered face.

### Moonfang Oracle
Support, Lunar. A shrine orphan trained in a moonlit sanctuary that honors the wolf as a protective emblem. She mends the living and speaks for the moon.

- **Base, Shrine Novice:** Plain grey shrine robes and a wooden charm.
- **Evo I, Night Seer:** Layered silver-trim robes and a dim lunar halo.
- **Evo II, Fang Priestess:** Crescent fang jewellery, floating ward talismans and luminous wolf-fang patterns on her mantle.
- **Evo III, Moonfang Oracle:** A sweeping crescent mantle, full moonlight, and crescent-shaped protective wards surrounding her.

### Crimson Eye Prodigy (**not summonable yet**)
Assassin, Fire. A prodigy who sealed her own sight, and carries a floating glass eye that shows her the path of a blade. It holds illusions rather than bloodline power.

- **Base, Veiled Student:** A dark red training coat and a plain cloth blindfold over both eyes.
- **Evo I, Glass Seer:** A single floating glass orb orbits her, showing what she can't see.
- **Evo II, Mirror Duelist:** A fitted long coat with silver stitching, and three glass orbs turning in a ring.
- **Evo III, Crimson Witness:** A tall collared mantle of red glass shards, a full ring of orbs, and an illusory second blade.

### Void Sovereign (**not summonable yet**)
Mage, Void. A scholar who opened a door nobody should, and came back wearing a crown that isn't quite there.

- **Base, Exiled Scholar:** A scuffed scholar's robe, ink-stained sleeves and a small dark orb.
- **Evo I, Riftwalker:** Hooded, with mirror-black shards drifting at the shoulders.
- **Evo II, Hollow Regent:** A high collar, a broken ring of shards, and the first hint of a crown.
- **Evo III, Void Sovereign:** A vast black mantle, a floating hollow crown, and a door of dark glass open behind him.

### Ashen Flame Lord (**not summonable yet**)
Mage, Fire. A warlord who burned his own banner to stop a war, and has been ashes and embers ever since.

- **Base, Banner Burner:** A soot-grey campaign robe, cracked gauntlets and a charred banner pole.
- **Evo I, Ember Marshal:** Blackened plates, with embers glowing through the seams.
- **Evo II, Pyre Warlord:** A horned iron helm and a torn burning cape.
- **Evo III, Ashen Flame Lord:** A crown of horns, a mantle of living ash and a field of pale cursed flame at his feet.

### Storm Crow Shinobi (**not summonable yet**)
Assassin, Storm. A mountain scout raised among storm-crows. A beak-masked blur who is gone before the thunder arrives.

- **Base, Ridge Fledgling:** A slate scout's wrap, a cloth face cover and short practice blades.
- **Evo I, Crow Runner:** A short feathered capelet and a single crow on the shoulder.
- **Evo II, Storm Talon:** A longer feather cloak and curved blades trailing blue sparks.
- **Evo III, Storm Crow:** Great storm-feather wings, a full beak helm, and afterimages trailing behind him.
