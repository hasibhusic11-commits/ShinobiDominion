# Castle Clash: buildings and modes

A research list, gathered 2026-11-05, for reference only. These are Castle Clash's names and features. Shinobi Dominion needs its own names and designs, so nothing here should be copied as is.

**Main source:** the fan-run [Castle Clash Handbook](https://en.casclash.com/handbook/buildings/). It shows no dates, and part of the modes list came from a summary of a truncated page, so names may be incomplete or out of date. The official site and the Fandom wiki refused automated access. Early-game descriptions come from a [2013 Gamezebo guide](https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/castle-clash-walkthrough/).

## What the main modes are, per guides
- **Dungeons:** attack pre-made enemy bases. Stages give rewards.
- **Raid:** attack other players' bases for resources. A 2022 guide says deploying units costs energy that regenerates. Shinobi Dominion's brief bans energy counters on the home screen and stamina gates on raids, so that needs care if combat copies the idea.
- **Here Be Monsters:** tower defense. Your base faces waves of AI heroes and troops.
- **Heroes Trial:** similar, against AI Legendary heroes.
- **Arena:** heroes charge along paths to destroy the other side's base. Earns Honor Badges.
- **Challenge a Boss:** a guild fights a very strong AI boss.
- **Fortress Feud:** guild teams of six heroes capture outposts and fortresses.
- **Castle Crisis:** a server-wide event ending in a boss stage.

The rest are named in the handbook, but I found no reliable description.
