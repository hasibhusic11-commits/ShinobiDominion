# Common and Elite heroes: ChatGPT image prompts

Owner's request, 2026-11-08: 10 Common and 10 Elite heroes that are easier to summon (Common 70%, Elite 28%,
Legendary 2%). These are the 20 heroes already in the game's data, with their names, roles, elements and concepts.

**One image per hero.** The same picture is used on the hero page and turned into the 3D model with Meshy, so it
must be a clean, neutral, full-body pose with no effects. (The Legendaries needed an extra clean-up step because
their paintings were full of flames and creatures; these won't.)

Save each image as `Downloads\SD_Heroes_CE\{hero_id}.png`, then tell Claude.

## Step 1: paste this first in a new ChatGPT chat, and attach one Legendary painting as the style reference

Attach `Downloads\SD_Heroes_final\solar_beast_sage.webp` (or any Legendary) with this message:

> You are drawing heroes for **Shinobi Dominion**, an original mobile strategy game set in a feudal-Japanese
> ninja world. Match the attached painting's art style exactly: polished hand-painted mobile hero-collector art,
> realistic adult proportions (about 7 heads tall), rich painterly shading, crisp edges, warm rim light, saturated
> but natural colours.
>
> Every image you make in this chat follows these rules:
> 1. **Full body, head to feet, front view**, the character standing in a neutral **A-pose**: arms straight and
>    angled down about 45 degrees away from the body, open relaxed hands (a weapon held firmly in one hand,
>    pointing down), legs straight at hip width, both feet flat and fully visible. Calm, confident face.
> 2. **No effects at all**: no fire, water, lightning, wind, glow, aura, smoke, sparks, energy, animals, birds,
>    spirits or anything floating. Only the character and what they wear and hold.
> 3. **Plain light grey background**, nothing else. Portrait 2:3. The character fills most of the height.
> 4. Every hero is original. Nothing from Naruto or any other anime, game or film: no forehead protectors, no
>    clan symbols, no copied costumes. No real people. No text, numbers or logos.
> 5. Their **rarity** shows in how elaborate they are. Common: simple practical clothes, one weapon, plain
>    colours. Elite: some armour, a signature prop and finer trim, but clearly less ornate than the attached
>    Legendary.
>
> Reply "Ready".

## Step 2: one message per hero

Copy each block, one at a time. If a picture breaks a rule (effects, cut-off feet, an action pose), reply
"Redo: full body, A-pose, no effects, plain grey background".
